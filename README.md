🔍 Overview

The goal of this project is to turn raw Vivo sales data into a clear, interactive dashboard that helps answer questions such as:

Which cities and regions generate the most sales and profit?
Which Vivo models are the most profitable?
Which months perform best or worst?
Which payment methods do customers prefer?
📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)
Metric	Value
Total Sales	575K
Total Profit	101K
Net Revenue	430M
Discount Values	0.25%

📈 Dashboard Components
Chart	Type	Description
Monthly Sales	Column chart	Month-by-month sales trend (Jan–Dec)
Category Wise Profit	Bar chart	Profit by model: Vivo Y56, Y200, Y100, X100, V30, V29, V27, T2
Region by Sales	Donut chart	Sales share across East, West, North, and South
City Wise Profit	Line chart	Profit comparison across 8 cities
Payment Type	Column chart	Sales split by Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI
City Wise Sale	Horizontal bar chart	Sales volume for each city
🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard uses Slicers so all charts and KPIs update together:

City: Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune
Payment: Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI
Region: East, North, South, West
Month: Jan – Dec (timeline/month slicer)
💡 Key Insights
Top models by profit: Vivo V29 (~13.23K), Vivo Y100 (~13.10K), and Vivo Y56 (~12.81K) lead in profit, while Vivo V30 trails (~11.72K).
Regional sales are well balanced: South (2,530), North (2,528), West (2,496), and East (2,446) are very close.
Strongest cities by sales: Bangalore (78K), Jaipur (77K), Delhi (74K), and Mumbai (74K).
Profit is steady across cities: most cities fall between 12K and 13K.
Payment preferences: Credit Card and UPI lead (~21K each), followed by Cash and Net Banking (~20K), with Debit Card lowest (~19K).
Monthly sales peak in January (886), May (867), and July (854), with the lowest in February (769).
